The Story

One Hundred Eighteen Years on the Water

Bay Haven Inn has stood on Bay Boulevard since 1908. Through prohibition, world wars, fishing booms and busts, it stayed open. The bar top is worn smooth. The walls are covered in the kind of history you can’t fake.

This is not a craft cocktail bar. It’s a place where fishermen and visitors sit on the same stools, where someone picks up a guitar on Sunday afternoon and the whole room joins in.

— Still pouring since 1908
