The Barber

Paul Runs This Chair

King’s Barber Shop is a one-man operation. Paul is the barber, the owner, and the reason people drive across town for a haircut. Old-school technique, clean fades, and the kind of unhurried attention you don’t get at chain shops.

He gets to know every client. Not just your hair—what you do, what you like, what you’re trying to look like.

What We Do

Classic Barbering, Done Right

01

Haircut

Precision cuts tailored to your style. Fades, tapers, classic side parts, textured crops—done with clippers, shears, and a steady hand.

02

Beard Trim

Shaped and cleaned up, from a quick edge-up to a full sculpt. Hot towel finish. Your beard, but sharper.

03

Straight Razor Shave

Hot lather, warm towel, straight blade. The way it was done before everything got fast and disposable.

04

Kids’ Cuts
