# Pitch Email Template

Use this template when reaching out to local businesses that don't have a website (or have a bad one). Replace the bracketed fields with business-specific details.

---

**Subject line options (pick one):**
- I built [BUSINESS NAME] a website — take a look
- Quick question about [BUSINESS NAME]'s online presence
- [BUSINESS NAME] deserves better than a Facebook page

---

**Email body:**

Hi [OWNER FIRST NAME],

I'm a web designer in Newport and I noticed [BUSINESS NAME] doesn't have a website yet. So I built you one — no strings attached, just to show you what's possible.

**Here's a live preview:** [DEMO URL]

Everything works — the booking form, the Google Maps, the mobile layout. It pulls in your real hours, address, phone number, and reviews.

A few things it does that a Facebook page can't:
- Shows up in Google when people search "[BUSINESS TYPE] in Newport"
- Loads in under 2 seconds on any phone
- Has a booking/reservation form built in
- Makes your business look as good online as it does in person

If you like it, I can connect your own domain and launch it for $500 flat — no hourly billing, no surprise fees. After that it's $30/month for hosting and support, cancel anytime.

If not, no worries at all. Either way the preview is yours to look at.

Best,
Will
[PHONE NUMBER]

---

## Personalization Notes

The key to this email is the demo link. Before sending:

1. **Research the business** — hours, address, phone, services, photos, social media
2. **Build the demo** — use the build pipeline to generate a site with their real info
3. **Deploy to GitHub Pages** — `luckygoats33.github.io/scan-pitch-[town]/[business-slug]/`
4. **Verify** — click every link, test mobile, run Lighthouse

The email works because you already did the work. You're not asking for a meeting or sending a generic pitch — you're showing them their own business looking incredible.

## Follow-up (3-5 days later, if no reply)

**Subject:** Re: [original subject]

Hi [OWNER FIRST NAME],

Just bumping this up — wanted to make sure you saw the website I put together for [BUSINESS NAME]. Here's the link again: [DEMO URL]

Happy to walk you through it in person if you'd like. I'm in Newport most days.

Will

## Finding Prospects

Look for businesses that:
- Have a Facebook page but no website
- Have a Google Business listing but no website linked
- Have an outdated website (Flash, broken links, not mobile-friendly)
- Are on Yelp with good reviews but no web presence
- Have a "website" that's just a Wix/Squarespace default template they never finished
